## 2026-03-25 - [Optimize Graph N+1 Queries with Batch Fetching]
**Learning:** SQLite's default variable limit (SQLITE_MAX_VARIABLE_NUMBER, often 999) can be a silent bottleneck when attempting to resolve N+1 queries. We discovered multiple N+1 patterns (e.g., sequentially resolving `get_node(qn)`) in `tools.py` during relationship expansion (`callers_of`, `callees_of`, etc.) and in `graph.py`'s impact analysis and subgraph extraction algorithms. Attempting to pass thousands of IDs directly into an `IN` clause fails without explicit batching.
**Action:** Always extract and deduplicate IDs/qualified_names into a list, and use a safe chunk size (e.g., 450) when executing `SELECT ... IN (?,...)`. To maintain output correctness matching the original list, map the retrieved rows back into the desired order.
